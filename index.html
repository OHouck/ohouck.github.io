Hi! I am a 5th year PhD Candidate at the University of Chicago's Harris School of Public Policy. My research focuses on understanding how innovations in weather forecasting can be used to improve climate adaptation and accelerate the transition to renewable energy. I enjoy working on interdisciplinary projects that combine methods from economics, climate science and machine learning. Please reach out if you would like to discuss research!
